Tier 3: advanced surrogates, generalized nonlinear least squares, and Bayesian optimization

Role of Tier 3

Tier 3 is explicit expert functionality. It is intended for scientifically justified model inadequacy, expensive experiments, complex smooth surfaces, or heterogeneous nonlinear errors. It is not an automatic replacement for the interpretable second-order response surface.

Example 1: GAM/thin-plate surrogate

method="gam" uses factor-specific thin-plate smooths plus pairwise tensor interactions; method="tps" uses a joint multivariate thin-plate smooth. Both are explicit alternatives rather than automatic replacements for the quadratic RSM.

gam_fit <- rsm_surrogate(d,"y",c("x1","x2"),method="gam")
rsm_surrogate_optimize(gam_fit,"max")
#> $solution
#>        x1        x2 
#> 0.6430193 0.1076355 
#> 
#> $predicted
#> [1] 1.7273
#> 
#> $goal
#> [1] "max"
#> 
#> $method
#> [1] "L-BFGS-B"
#> 
#> $surrogate
#> [1] "gam"

A thin-plate variant can be selected with method="tps". Both are fitted with mgcv and remain outside the classical canonical-analysis workflow.

rsm_bayes_opt() proposes one next experimental point from the current data. It never fabricates that future response and therefore cannot iterate without the user supplying the new observation.

Example 3: random forest and neural network sensitivity models

rf <- rsm_surrogate(d,"y",c("x1","x2"),method="rf")
rsm_surrogate_optimize(rf,"max")
#> $solution
#>        x1        x2 
#> 0.6058129 0.1183170 
#> 
#> $predicted
#> [1] 1.521822
#> 
#> $goal
#> [1] "max"
#> 
#> $method
#> [1] "L-BFGS-B"
#> 
#> $surrogate
#> [1] "rf"
nn <- rsm_surrogate(d,"y",c("x1","x2"),method="nn",size=5,decay=.01)
rsm_surrogate_optimize(nn,"max")
#> $solution
#>         x1         x2 
#> 0.66206718 0.06156311 
#> 
#> $predicted
#> [1] 1.653495
#> 
#> $goal
#> [1] "max"
#> 
#> $method
#> [1] "L-BFGS-B"
#> 
#> $surrogate
#> [1] "nn"

These models are primarily predictive/sensitivity surrogates. Their use should be reported separately from polynomial canonical interpretation.

Generalized nonlinear least squares as Tier 3

For mechanistic nonlinear surfaces with heterogeneous or correlated error, engine="gnls" provides a separate route through nlme. The mean structure remains explicit, while variance or correlation structures are declared by the analyst.
