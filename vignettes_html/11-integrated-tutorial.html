Introduction

0.1 What is rsmFlow?

The rsmFlow package provides a design-aware response-surface workflow for teaching and advanced scientific use.

This tutorial walks through five scientific scenarios:

  1. Classical RSM — polynomial surface, canonical analysis, optimization
  2. Optimization and uncertainty — near-optimal regions, economic optimum
  3. GLM-RSM — Poisson, Binomial, Gamma responses
  4. Nonlinear surfaces — Mitscherlich, Michaelis-Menten with GA initialization
  5. Advanced surrogates — GP, GAM, random forest, neural network, Bayesian optimization
